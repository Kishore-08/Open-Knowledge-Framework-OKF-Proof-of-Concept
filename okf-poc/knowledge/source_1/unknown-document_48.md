32. Difference between Role, ClusterRole, 
RoleBinding, ClusterRoleBinding 
Answer 
Role 
Namespace-scoped permissions. 
Access only inside one namespace 
 
ClusterRole 
Cluster-wide permissions. 
Access across all namespaces or cluster resources 
 
RoleBinding 
Attaches Role to a user/service account (namespace scoped). 
 
ClusterRoleBinding 
Attaches ClusterRole globally.
