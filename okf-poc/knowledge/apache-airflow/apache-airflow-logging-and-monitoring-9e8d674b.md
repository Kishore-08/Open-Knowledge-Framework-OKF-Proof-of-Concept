### Logging and Monitoring

You can extend the way how logs are written by Airflow. You can find out more about log writing in
[Logging & Monitoring](https://airflow.apache.org/docs/apache-airflow/stable/administration-and-deployment/logging-monitoring/index.html).

The [Writing logs](https://airflow.apache.org/docs/apache-airflow-providers/core-extensions/logging.html "(in apache-airflow-providers vstable)") that also shows available log writers
implemented in the community providers.
