### Extra Links

When creating Hooks, you can add custom Extra Links that are displayed when the tasks are run.
You can find out more about extra links in [Extra Links](https://airflow.apache.org/docs/apache-airflow-providers/core-extensions/extra-links.html "(in apache-airflow-providers vstable)")
that also shows available extra links implemented in the community providers.
