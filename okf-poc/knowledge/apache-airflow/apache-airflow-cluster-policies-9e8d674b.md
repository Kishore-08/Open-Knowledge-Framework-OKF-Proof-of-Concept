### Cluster Policies

Cluster Policies are the way to dynamically apply cluster-wide policies to the Dags being parsed or tasks
being executed. You can read more about Cluster Policies in [Cluster Policies](https://airflow.apache.org/docs/apache-airflow/stable/administration-and-deployment/cluster-policies.html).
