### Connections

When creating Hooks, you can add custom Connections. You can read more
about connections in [Connections](https://airflow.apache.org/docs/apache-airflow-providers/core-extensions/connections.html "(in apache-airflow-providers vstable)") for available
Connections implemented in the community providers.
