## Scaling cluster infrastructure

If scaling workloads isn't enough to meet your needs, you can also scale your cluster infrastructure itself.

Scaling the cluster infrastructure normally means adding or removing [nodes](https://kubernetes.io/docs/concepts/architecture/nodes/ "A node is a worker machine in Kubernetes.").
Read [Node autoscaling](https://kubernetes.io/docs/concepts/cluster-administration/node-autoscaling/)
for more information.
