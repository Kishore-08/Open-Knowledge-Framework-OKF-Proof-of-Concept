## *Runtime* lifecycle phase

The Runtime phase comprises three critical areas: [access](https://kubernetes.io/docs/concepts/security/cloud-native-security/#protection-runtime-access),
[compute](https://kubernetes.io/docs/concepts/security/cloud-native-security/#protection-runtime-compute), and [storage](https://kubernetes.io/docs/concepts/security/cloud-native-security/#protection-runtime-storage).
